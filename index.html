Ayoub Safi

Instructional Designer

I spent four years teaching English in UNRWA classrooms, grades 1 to 6, before I designed a single course. I moved into instructional design a little over a year ago, and the classroom is still how I work: I start from what a learner actually does with the material, not what the slide says.

In that year I've designed e-learning for both young learners and workplace teams. That includes a digital awareness course for ages 6 to 18, branching scenarios for professional skills, and many other projects. Day to day I work in Articulate Storyline 360, Rise 360 and PowerPoint, and I'm deepening my practice in scenario-based learning.

I'm early in this career, and I bring a teacher's habit of checking whether the lesson landed. What I care about most is whether the learner can do the thing afterwards.
